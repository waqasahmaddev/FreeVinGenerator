Which Tool Do You Need?

Each tool solves a different problem. Here is the quick version:

  • Need fake-but-valid VINs for testing? Use the VIN Generator for one at a time, or the Bulk Generator when you need hundreds for a database or test suite.
  • Have a real VIN and want its details? The VIN Decoder reads the country, manufacturer, model year, and check digit from any 17-character VIN.
  • Just need to confirm a number is real? The VIN Validator checks the length, the characters, and the check-digit math in a second.
  • Managing inventory or a workshop? The QR Code Generator turns a VIN into a scannable code you can print or share.
  • Learning how VINs work? The Breakdown Visualizer maps every character to its meaning, section by section.

Are These Tools Free?

Yes. Every tool on this page is free to use, with no account, no limits, and no payment. We do not store the VINs you enter or generate. The generator produces structurally valid numbers with correct check digits, meant for software testing, development, and learning, never for registration, insurance, or any legal use.
